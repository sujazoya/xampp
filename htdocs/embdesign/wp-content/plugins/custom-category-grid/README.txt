=== Custom Category Grid for WooCommerce ===

Description:
Use the shortcode [custom_categories_grid] to display WooCommerce product categories in a styled animated grid, excluding "Bundles" and its subcategories.

Installation:
1. Upload the folder to /wp-content/plugins/
2. Activate from WordPress > Plugins
3. Use the shortcode anywhere in content

Styles: Matches your design with green rounded card, image on black background, hover animation, and mobile responsiveness.
